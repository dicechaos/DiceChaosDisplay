## Dice Chaos Display — Initial Release (`v1.0.0`)

Welcome to the first official release of **Dice Chaos Display** by **[Dice Chaos](https://dicechaos.com)**!

**Dice Chaos Display** is an offline-first 4K/8K battlemap studio, true 1-inch physical miniature calibrator, and spoiler-safe **2nd-Screen Tabletop TV Display** built to pair seamlessly alongside **[Dice Chaos Connections](https://github.com/dicechaos/DiceChaosConnections)** and **[Dice Chaos Masters](https://github.com/dicechaos/DiceChaosMasters)** in the **Dice Chaos Tabletop Suite**—engineered for Dungeon Masters, Game Masters, and in-person tabletop groups rolling real **[Dice Chaos](https://dicechaos.com)** polyhedral dice and moving physical miniatures on TV glass.

> **Part of the Dice Chaos Tabletop Suite:** Forge custom polyhedral dice masters & matched insert blanks in **[Dice Chaos Masters](https://github.com/dicechaos/DiceChaosMasters)**, map out your campaign's NPCs, factions, and conspiracy webs in **[Dice Chaos Connections](https://github.com/dicechaos/DiceChaosConnections)**, and run locked-60-FPS 4K/8K tactical encounters on your table TV in **Dice Chaos Display**!

### What's Inside `v1.0.0`
- **Zero-Spoiler Dual-Screen DM Studio & Smart `F12` TV Toggle:** Stage maps, fog, and secret notes privately on your DM screen before pushing (`Ctrl+Enter`), toggle the Player TV **ON / OFF** anytime (`F12` or `Esc` on TV) without losing DM focus, engage instant **Blackout / Standby Splash** (`B`), and prep safely on a single laptop screen via automatic **16:9 Floating Preview Mode**.
- **True 1-Inch (`25.4 mm`) Physical Miniature Calibration:** Per-Monitor V2 High-DPI scale math with 1-tap TV diagonal presets (`32"`–`65"`) plus a live **On-TV 1-Inch Physical Ruler** so real miniature bases match map squares 1:1 across `0° / 90° / 180° / 270°` table orientations.
- **60 FPS 4K / 8K Battlemap, Video & Universal VTT Engine:** Frustum-culled `2048×2048` GPU tiles, airspace-free looping **`MP4` / `WEBM` / `MKV` video battlemaps** rendered smoothly beneath overlays and fog, and 1-step **`.dd2vtt` / `.uvtt` / `.df2vtt`** import with automatic grid alignment and DM-only wall/door guides.
- **Sub-Millisecond Feathered Fog of War & 1-Click Dungeon Rooms:** Zero-copy SkiaSharp `Fog Brush`, `Fog Box`, and `Fog Polygon` tools with soft feathered edges, `Right-Drag` inverse painting, translucent DM fog preview (`100%` opaque black on TV), and pre-pped **1-Click Room** reveal badges.
- **Under-Miniature Glow Rings & 5e Tactical Overlays:** Clutter-free neon rings built to shine underneath physical miniatures (*Concentrating*, *Bloodied*, *Blessed*, *Hexed*, *Poisoned*, *Prone*, *10ft–30ft Auras*), rotatable `Sphere`, `5e Cone`, `Cube`, and `Line` spell templates, an interactive **3×3 Visual Grid Matcher**, a `5-5-5` / `5-10-5` / `Euclidean` distance ruler, `Alt+Click` sonar pings, and **`🔒 Secret DM Note Pins`**.
- **Pre-Staged Handout Gallery & Local `.dicechaos` Ownership:** Project NPC portraits, monster art, and puzzle handouts onto your rotated TV in one click, with unified multi-domain Undo/Redo (`Ctrl+Z` / `Ctrl+Y`) and 100% local `.dicechaos` campaign files.
